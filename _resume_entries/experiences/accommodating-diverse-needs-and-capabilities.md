Through various roles as a collaborator, leader, or student, I have encountered and worked with people with diverse needs and abilities. Differences such as physical or mental limitations due to injury or disease, significantly different life experiences, or even just being in distinct demographics, have come up for me as a peer mentor, a coach, and as a member of various teams. Learning how to work with people who approach problems very differently has been a humbling experience. I have great respect for people who must overcome such different, and often poorly understood, obstacles to achieve their goals. I look forward to improving my own awareness of the challenges of different communities.
