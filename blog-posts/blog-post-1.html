Exploring Architecture and Design

October 27, 2023  •  Shilong Pan

Architecture is more than buildings — it's about how spaces shape human experience. In this post I reflect on recent walks through downtown Vancouver, noting how material choices, light, and proportion influence mood and movement.

From the glass facades of modern towers to the brick textures of heritage blocks, each detail tells a story about the era it was built in and the people who use it daily.

Key takeaways: pay attention to scale, respect context, and design for people first.
